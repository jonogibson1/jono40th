Drinks · someone to pour

Cocktail bartender

BYO is the move for a weekend like this: you buy the alcohol yourself (Dan Murphy's or a wholesaler, far cheaper than any bar's markup) and hire staff who bring the bar, glassware, ice, garnishes and mixers and pour all night. Byron BYO operators:

Torley's Bar Services - region's highest-rated, 200+ five-star reviews, explicit BYO cocktail and BYO beverage packages. Ph 0422 542 336.
Barmen For Hire - hire bartenders by the hour (BYO) or as a full package, plus a 60-90 min mixology masterclass option.
Flossy Byron Bay - BYO mobile bar, you supply alcohol, they do the rest; fun fairy-floss cocktail gimmick for the party.
Fern (via Wedlockers) - eco pop-up bar for Byron/Northern NSW, $300-450/hr, min 2 hours, includes 2 staff, glassware and styling.

Ballpark for BYO: roughly $300-450/hr for two staff, minimum two hours, plus whatever you spend on the grog.
Torley's BYO Barmen Flossy

Kids · day out

Byron Wildlife Sanctuary

Not a hire, an escape valve. The Byron Bay Wildlife Sanctuary (old Macadamia Castle) has a mini-golf course, water park, kids play area and animal encounters (snakes, lizards, owls, sugar gliders). Half a day out with the kids if the house needs a reset, about 20 min from Nashua.

Recovery · Sunday

Poolside massage

The plan: book therapists for a 4-hour block and let them rotate. You pay for the time, not per person. One therapist doing 30-min sessions gets through ~8 people in 4 hours; two therapists working side by side clear the whole group comfortably. Everyone else is in the pool waiting their turn.

Byron operators that do group bookings and cover Bangalow / Federal (next to Nashua):
Byron Bay Mobile Massage - coordinates multiple therapists for group bookings, call ahead.
Beachside Yoga & Massage - mobile to Bangalow/Federal, group discounts, cash or transfer.
Byron Health Studio - premium mobile, explicitly does curated group bodywork for private events.
Blys - app-based, book multiple therapists to an address for a set time.

Ballpark: roughly $120-160 per therapist per hour, so a 4-hour two-therapist block is about $960-1,280. Sunday morning after the big night is the most-appreciated booking of the weekend.
Byron Mobile Byron Health Blys

The Byron special

Sound journey & cacao

The legal end of the "expanded states" spectrum, and Byron does it better than anywhere. A facilitator brings gongs, singing bowls and a ceremonial cacao for a guided sound journey on the deck, deep relaxation without anything that gets anyone arrested or scares the kids. Rise Temple and the OM Byron network run these as private group sessions. Ballpark: $250 to $600 for the group.

For friends not sleeping at the house

Stay nearby

The Hacienda sleeps 12 and that's a firm cap. If you want to come, book your own bed close by and roll over for the day: pool, food, party, then back to your place to sleep. Cluster around Bangalow (10 min) so there's a town with cafes and restaurants for the morning-after. Daytime party guests are welcome, Jono is clearing the numbers and event fee with the host, so let him know if you're coming.

Closest · same village

Nashua & Newrybar

Minutes from The Hacienda. The Cottage (renovated 1890s coach house on a 120-acre farm) and Blackwood (2BR timber tiny home, walk to Newrybar village and Harvest). A handful of eco villas and farm studios around here too. Best for a couple or small family who want to be right next door.

Most beds · 10 min

Bangalow

The overflow HQ. Walkable town, cafes and restaurants, and the most places to stay. Carinya (six modern villas, cluster a few families in one spot), Bangalow Guesthouse (heritage rooms, three cottages and an apartment on one property), and plenty of houses and cottages. Point most people here.

Federal & Possum Creek

More hinterland cottages and farm stays if the closer towns fill up. A little more spread out and rural, good for anyone after peace and a view rather than walking distance to a cafe.

Lennox Head & Ballina

If some of the crew would rather wake up near the beach than in the hills, Lennox and Ballina are a short drive and have motels, apartments and houses across all budgets. Easy run over to Nashua for the day.
