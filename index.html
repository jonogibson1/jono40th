How a house made this list

Every property below survived five gates, applied in order. Fail one, gone.

Gate 1

Available

Live-searched on Airbnb and Stayz on 16 August 2026 with the actual dates loaded, Fri 26 to Mon 29 March 2027. Dated searches only return bookable houses, so presence is proof. Easter Saturday is the most contested booking night of the year; most of Queensland already failed this gate.
Gate 2

Fits 12

Three families, twelve people at most. Under 10 beds needed a good excuse; over 20 meant paying for air.
Gate 3

Pool

Non-negotiable and filtered at source. The pool is load-bearing infrastructure for the Caesar programme, detailed below.
Gate 4

Two hours of Brisbane

Measured from Ferny Hills. One exception was granted five extra minutes for being in the Byron hinterland and cheap.
Gate 5

Verified

Prices captured live including taxes and fees, cancellation terms read from the listing, and all 56 photos checked against the source before embedding. One dead image was caught and replaced. Nothing here is decorative guesswork.
The instrument that ranks them

The Caesar Index

The critical measure. It scores one thing: can the host of this event drink a Caesar by the pool at 7am in the nood without consequence. One celery stick per condition met. Cameras were ruled an acceptable audience and do not affect the score.

No sightlines. The pool is not overlooked by a road, a public track, or a neighbouring deck.
No residents. No host, caretaker or occupied dwelling on the property beyond your own party.
Genuine isolation. The nearest uninvolved human is a paddock, a valley or a national park away. Only Eighteen Mile holds all three.
